The Name

Chico answered his whole life to a name that never made it onto paper. Episode one is how he got the others.

July 2024. A U-Haul running warm out of Big Stone Gap, Virginia. Somewhere past the state line, I reached over and hit record.

Chico was born Larry Pendergrass Jr., named for a father shot dead before he was born. At eighteen he stood in a courthouse and made Larry Huff legal. Chico, the name everybody called him, never made it onto paper.

Episode one is the names: where each one came from, who handed it to him, and the man he called Dad, who sent him to kindergarten already writing cursive so he wouldn't end up “an old bum like me.”

With Mack Greene, Homer, and Dr. Lloyd Tomlinson of UVA Wise.

Content note
Strong language, a fatal shooting, and a description of child abuse.
